## Scope
Home page and the inner pages that share its system (servicios, servicio detail, proyectos, nosotros, contacto). Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience and job
Homeowners and administrators of conjuntos/empresas in Villavicencio and the Meta. Job: confirm this company is serious and has done work like theirs, then request a quote by WhatsApp.

## Direction contract
THESIS: An institutional company profile, like a well-made corporate brochure for a contractor that has been around for 30 years. It refuses the dark full-bleed video hero with gradient headline, the eyebrow-over-every-heading rhythm and the stat-counter band.
OWN-WORLD: Light cool-grey ground (#F4F6F5) and white surfaces; deep petrol green (#0F3B33) owns large rounded panels (radius 28px) set inside the page margins; near-black green text (#0E1A17); lime (#8DB33A) appears only as a tiny accent (focus ring, a rule, an active marker). Schibsted Grotesk for headings, Geist for text. Pill buttons. Phosphor icons at one weight. Real project photography in rounded frames. No gradients anywhere.
STORY: The visitor sees a real door opening, learns in one line what the company does and where, sees the people and 30 years behind it, picks the service that matches their door, sees real installed work, and writes on WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: H1 on two lines, a 20-word subline, primary pill "Cotizar por WhatsApp" plus a text link to proyectos. Right column (about 55% width): the roller-shutter video inside a rounded frame, revealed once with a clip-path inset. Header above: logo, nav in a pill group, the same CTA.
FORM: Pinned by the owner (references plus palette plus framed hero); not rolled. Seed key: none (brief-pinned).
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Services index as a list of rows on the left; hovering or selecting a row swaps the large photo on the right with a clip-path reveal and shows that service's short answer. Accordion on mobile.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
