## Platform

web

## Users
Two audiences with equal weight, confirmed by the owner:
- Homeowners and finca owners in Villavicencio and the Meta who want to automate a sliding, swing or garage door, or need a repair.
- Administrators of residential complexes (conjuntos), companies, commerce and builders who need talanqueras, access control, and recurring maintenance.
Both arrive mostly from Google search or Google Maps on a phone and decide by contacting through WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose
Puertas Automáticas del Llano installs, automates, maintains and repairs automatic and electric doors (corredizas, abatibles, levadizas/seccionales, cortinas enrollables), talanqueras and access control. The site exists to rank locally (SEO and AI search) and turn visits into WhatsApp quote requests or calls.

## Positioning
More than 30 years automating doors in the Meta, led by the founder Carlos Javier Guarama Otero, an electronics expert who diagnoses the real fault (motors, boards, sensors) instead of swapping parts. Works with all major motor brands.

## Operating Context
Contact happens through WhatsApp (+57 312 385 4010), phone and email (puertasautomaticas@gmail.com). Hours: Monday to Saturday, 7:00 a. m. to 6:00 p. m. Base in Villavicencio, Meta. Typical request: a photo of the door plus approximate measurements, then a technical visit and a quote.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Services: corredizas, abatibles, levadizas y seccionales, cortinas enrollables, talanqueras, control de acceso (electroimanes, lectores, programación de controles), mantenimiento y reparación.
- Brands worked: Nice, BFT, FAAC, CAME, Beninca, LiftMaster, Genius, PPA, Accessmatic, NAS; access control ZKTeco, Dahua, Hikvision, Soyal.
- Existing WordPress URLs (/, /nosotros/, /servicios/, /proyectos/, /contacto/) must be preserved for SEO.
- Coverage municipalities beyond Villavicencio are unconfirmed (current list is an assumption to validate).
- Stack: Next.js 16 (App Router, static), Tailwind CSS 4, Motion.

## Brand Commitments
- Name and logo (isometric cube in blue, teal, green and lime) stay as they are.
- The owner wants an institutional, serious, elegant and modern look. No gradients. References: a construction company site in deep green with rounded contained panels, a law-firm site in dark teal with a single warm accent, and a clean renewable-energy template.
- Values stated by the company: seriedad y honestidad as the two pillars; satisfacción, ética, colaboración, excelencia.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real project photos and videos from the current site in public/proyectos and public/video (sliding, swing, sectional garage, talanqueras, access control, technicians at work, the founder at a talanquera).
- Founder statement (paraphrased from the current site) and mission/vision text.
- No testimonials, ratings, project counts or client logos are available. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Contacting the company by WhatsApp must be possible from every screen in one tap.
2. Show real installed work before making claims.
3. Speak plainly to both a homeowner and an administrator; no hype.
4. Local relevance first: Villavicencio and the Meta named where it helps people and search engines.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Mostly mobile visitors, often outdoors in strong light: high contrast, large tap targets, readable body size (17px+).
